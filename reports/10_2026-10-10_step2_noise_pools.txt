1. PURPOSE
  SimCLR adds real background noise to each training view. In a federated
  setting a client may only use noise recorded on its own days. Protocol v2
  therefore requires every training event, in every arm, to draw noise only
  from its own day. The reviewer (M1) found that 5 needed days had no pool:
  Feb 7, 13, 19 and 25 (client days in the fold design) and Feb 10 (tuning
  and monitoring).

2. RULE (fixed in report 09 before this step ran)
  - Strict pool: rule I5 of ssl_central/bgpool.py, with committee C's
    per-day block choice (6 blocks per day, evenly spaced, deterministic).
    A stretch is rejected if any detection of any SNR lies within 3 s, if it
    contains a dropout flag, or if it exceeds 6 robust sigma.
  - If a day has fewer than N_MIN = 50 strict segments, its relaxed segments
    are added (up to 10 % flagged samples, bridged; detections still veto).
  - Clarification, made before building the new pools: the relaxed rule also
    accepts every strict-clean stretch, so segments are de-duplicated by
    (run, position), keeping the strict copy.

3. PROCEDURE
  - Committee C's functions were reused unchanged: the planner
    noisepool.plan, the strict job ssl_central.bgpool._job and the relaxed
    job noisepool._job_relaxed. Only the list of days was changed.
  - The new segments were merged with C's existing pool, which was read
    only; C's files are unchanged.
  - Output rows are sorted by day, run and position.
  - Runtime: 7 min 12 s on 12 CPU processes; no GPU.
