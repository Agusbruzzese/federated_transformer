  contrast).
    C1   C256rep(10-19) vs C256(0-9)                      EQUIVALENT
    C3   INIT(0-9) vs C256(0-9)                           INFERIOR

    L2   per-evaluation-day offsets kappa in {0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.03,

  Noise floor (descriptive): 15-NN overlap between C256 seed s and C256rep

A3. ANALYSIS CODE FROZEN BEFORE TRAINING (R2)
  - score_all.py dryrun|main produces tidy tables scores_* and models_*
    (arm, seed, fold, day, metric, value; and model-level effective rank and
    gate statistics). The main analysis uses the concatenation of the
    dryrun and main tables (C256 rows come from the dry run).
  - analyze.py controls|main implements:
      - report 09 sections 3.5 and 5.3-5.9, and section A2;
      - Q-F; Q-S (Holm over 4 one-sided tests; HELPS / DOES NOT HELP /
        INCONCLUSIVE);
      - the three attribution rules;
      - the interaction and the seed-paired version (section A4);
      - collapse; sensitivity analyses (a)-(d).
    Every grid is built by grid(). It asserts the exact seed set, the
    canonical 9-day order from folds.json, one value per cell, finite
    values, and that each day was scored by a model of that day's fold.
    Misaligned or incomplete input raises; row order is irrelevant (tested).
  - evaluator2.py, used with evaluator.py:
      - refuses to score a checkpoint on a fold it was not trained for
        (server checkpoints excepted);
      - computes effective rank;
      - asserts >= 2 classes per day;
      - counts non-converged gate fits.
  - tune.py scores the tuning runs on Feb 10 (probes fitted on fold-0
    client events) and applies the report 09 section 4 rule.

A4. FORMULAS NOT GIVEN IN REPORT 09
  - Seed-paired two-stratum t (sensitivity (c)):
      seed stratum  var_s(M_A(s) - M_B(s)) / n,  df n - 1
      day stratum   as in report 09 section 5.3
      Satterthwaite df; 90 % CI.
  - Interaction (S+F - F-nat) - (S+C - C256):
      I(s) = M_SF(s) - M_F(s) - M_SC(s) + M_C(s)  (seeds shared)
      seed stratum  var(I) / n,  df n - 1
      c_d = the same 4-arm contrast of day-d seed means
      day stratum   var(c_d - mean c) / D,  df D - 1
      Satterthwaite df; 90 % CI.

A5. CORRECTIONS TO REPORT 09
  - Section 1.4 and section 8.1: the simulator is protocol/fedsim3.py; the
    code controls are in test_step5b.py (C0, C0') and test_fedsim2.py
    (history).
  - Section 8.2: L1 is NOT "PASS by construction". Reviewer F simulated a
    false-FAIL rate of about 0 at the measured Z spread (SD about 0.008),
    0.019 at SD 0.012 and 0.26 at SD 0.02. The response is pre-stated in A2.
  - Section 5.3 caveat (kept in the claims): days of one fold share a model,
    so with an arm x fold interaction the day stratum understates the SE.
    The partition-dependence flag is the protection; it is reported with
    every contrast.
  - The IID permutation (seed 1000 + fold) is the same for every init seed.
  - Warm-up with fractional steps overshoots the peak learning rate by at
    most 0.17 % (C64, F-X+P). This is negligible and kept.
  - folds.py writes folds.json in "w" mode. It must not be rerun:
    folds.json is pinned by hash in fedsim3 (79f3b1ff...).
