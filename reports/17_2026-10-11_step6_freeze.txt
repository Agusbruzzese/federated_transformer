3. NORMATIVE CLARIFICATION (reviewer F, item F3)
  Scope of the sensitivity analyses (report 09 section 5.9):
    (a) fold 0 only, (b) without collapsed runs and (d) without Feb 7 are
        applied to the confirmatory question Q-F (F-nat vs C256) only;
    (c) seed-paired inference is applied to Q-F and to Q-S.
  In (b), a seed flagged as collapsed in ANY fold is removed from that arm
  for the whole contrast, because the seed x day grid must be complete.
  If fewer than 2 seeds remain, (b) is "NOT ESTIMABLE".
  The collapse rule (report 09 section 3.5) is applied to the trained arms
  (C256, C64, F-IID, F-nat, S+F, S+C, F-X+P, CJ). Server checkpoints and S0
  are descriptive only; a collapsed server checkpoint is reported together
  with the S+F and S+C runs that depend on it.
  Sensitivity results never change a primary label; they are reported next
  to it.
