 CENTRAL VS FEDERATED (10 CLIENTS) VS SERVER-PRETRAINED FEDERATED
 SELF-SUPERVISED TRAINING OF A MAGNETIC-SIGNATURE ENCODER
 Report 24: final report (step 13)
 Project: federated_transformer                Date: 2026-10-12
 Protocol: v2.1, frozen 2026-10-11 (reports 09 + 14 + 17)
 Results verified by independent recomputation (report 23)

SUMMARY
-------
Question. Does training the same self-supervised encoder by federated
averaging across 10 clients (one client = one or two recording days) give a
representation equivalent to central training on the same events, under
equal conditions? Does pretraining on the server's own days help the
federated model?

Answers (pre-registered decision rules, 10 seeds, 9 unseen evaluation days):
  1. FEDERATED vs CENTRAL: EQUIVALENT within the pre-set margins.
       lobe count  -0.023  [-0.034, -0.012]  (margin +-0.04)
       polarity    -0.010  [-0.015, -0.004]  (margin +-0.02)
     Federated is measurably a little lower (both CIs exclude 0), but
     within the margins. It encodes no more day-specific nuisance than
     central (slightly less).
  2. SERVER PRETRAINING: HELPS, on lobe structure.
       +0.049 over plain federated, +0.038 over federated given the same
       extra compute (Holm-corrected, p < 1e-5); no effect on polarity.
       Pretraining helps the federated model more than the central one
       (interaction +0.038 [+0.021, +0.055]). The server-pretrained
       federated model is non-inferior to central, and above it on lobes
       (+0.026).
  3. Every number was reproduced exactly by an independent reviewer with
     separate code (900 of 900 score cells identical).

Main caveat. The pre-registered central reference used batch 256. A central
variant with batch 64 scored higher on lobes (+0.035). Plain federated is
about one lobe margin below that variant (descriptive, not confirmatory).
The equivalence holds against the pre-registered reference, not against the
best central variant seen.

What the claims cover. Waveform-shape probes (lobe count, polarity) derived
from morphology: there are no vehicle labels. The scope is these 3 day-based
partitions, one TMR sensor at one site, 14 winter days, and SNR >= 20
events. All results are exploratory with respect to the dataset, which had
been used in earlier phases.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY THIS STUDY, AND HOW IT WAS MADE TRUSTWORTHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The project trains a self-supervised (SimCLR) encoder on magnetic vehicle
signatures, with the aim of federated deployment. Earlier phases (reports
02-07) showed that the central encoder learns waveform structure, but they
never compared it fairly with federated training.

Process:
  - A committee of four independent agents examined the central model and
    designed the comparison:
      A  analyst, measured the model;
      B  statistician;
      C  federated engineer;
      D  validity critic.
    Two further independent reviewers checked the protocol and code
    (E, F), and one recomputed the results (G). Each report is saved
    verbatim in research/model/K_committee/.
  - The protocol was frozen by sha256 before any study model was trained
    (report 17). Every later step verified the freeze.
  - Bugs found and fixed BEFORE any result existed:
      - the original simulator trained clients sequentially instead of
        averaging them (E);
      - a shared noise pool crossed client boundaries (D);
      - the training loop hung on small clients (D);
      - a gate-averaging bug in the analysis (test suite);
      - three code/protocol mismatches (F).
  - Planted controls proved the decision procedure could both pass and fail
    on real models before the federated models were scored (report 19).
  - The independent recomputation reproduced every number (report 23).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - 4 375 vehicle events with SNR >= 20 from one TMR sensor (14 days, 4 runs,
    Feb 2025). 12 s waveforms at 50 Hz, max-normalised (shape only).
  - Server days: Feb 18 + Feb 20 (434 events), used only by the server.
  - Tuning day: Feb 10, used only for learning-rate selection.
  - 3-fold cross-fitting over 9 evaluation days:
      fold 0: Feb 5, 11, 25
      fold 1: Feb 6, 13, 26
      fold 2: Feb 7, 14, 27
    Every evaluation day is scored only by models that never trained on it.
  - Per fold, the remaining days form exactly 10 clients (53-434 events each;
    2 537-2 763 training events per fold).
  - Every training event draws background noise only from its own day, in
    every arm, so no client touches another client's data.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT WAS COMPARED (all arms on the same client events, same encoder)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Encoder: patch transformer, 207k parameters. SimCLR NT-Xent (T = 0.1,
  30 s false-negative guard). M5 augmentations. AdamW, 5 % warm-up + cosine.
  Budget X = 2.56 M training examples.

  arm     what                                         lr (tuned)  budget
  ------  -------------------------------------------  ----------  ------
  C256    central, batch 256: REFERENCE                1.5e-4      X
  C64     central, batch 64 (fewer negatives)          3e-4        X
  F-nat   FedAvg, 10 natural day-clients, 200 rounds   3e-4        X
  F-IID   FedAvg, 10 IID clients of the same sizes     3e-4        X
  F-X+P   FedAvg with the server's extra compute,      3e-4        X+P
          random init
  S+F     server pretraining (P = 424 k) then F-nat    1e-3        P+X
  S+C     server pretraining then central batch 256    3e-4        P+X
  CJ      central on clients + server days             1e-3        X+P
  Every trained arm type received the same tuning budget (4 learning rates,
  frozen selection rule; report 18). Each arm was trained for 10 seeds x 3
  folds: 240 models, plus 30 replicate central models and 30 ablation
  models.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW "EQUIVALENT" WAS DECIDED (fixed in advance)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Probes (kNN and linear) fitted on each fold's client-event embeddings,
    scored per unseen day. Primary metrics: lobe count and polarity (mean
    of kNN and linear skill).
  - Margins from the central model's own variability, each at most 1/4 of
    what SSL learns over an untrained network: lobes +-0.04, polarity
    +-0.02.
  - Two-stratum t (seed and day variation), 90 % CI. EQUIVALENT if both
    primary CIs lie inside the margins. Server pretraining "HELPS" if, on
    some metric, it beats both plain FedAvg and equal-compute FedAvg
    (Holm-corrected) by at least half a margin.
  - Nuisance-encoding gate: how much information about the day an event
    comes from the embedding holds, beyond the event's physical description,
    against a permutation null. Margin 0.03.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROOF THAT THE PROCEDURE WORKS (dry run on real central models,
   report 19)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  control                              required        observed (lobes)
  -----------------------------------  --------------  ------------------------
  central vs independent central       EQUIVALENT      +0.003 [-0.008, +0.014]
  untrained network                    INFERIOR        -0.324
  heavily noised model                 INFERIOR        -0.097
  degraded just past the margin        not equivalent  -0.041 -> INCONCLUSIVE
  planted day signal (gate)            flagged         +0.122 -> FAIL
  All passed. Two independent central groups differ by only +0.003, so the
  procedure had room to declare equivalence; and a model about one margin
  worse was refused.

6. RESULTS (report 22; reproduced in report 23)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Mean scores (10 seeds x 9 unseen days)
  arm      lobes   polarity  duration
  C64      0.635   0.916     0.842
  CJ       0.628   0.914     0.819
  S+F      0.626   0.908     0.855
  S+C      0.611   0.914     0.836
  C256     0.600   0.914     0.839    <- reference
  F-X+P    0.587   0.904     0.850
  F-IID    0.586   0.903     0.853
  F-nat    0.577   0.904     0.850
  S0       0.555   0.901     0.848    (server model alone)
  untrained 0.276  0.756     0.769

6.2 Confirmatory question 1: F-nat vs C256 -> EQUIVALENT, gate PASS
  lobes -0.023 [-0.034, -0.012]; polarity -0.010 [-0.015, -0.004];
  consistent across folds (-0.025 / -0.023 / -0.020). Nuisance gate:
  federated encodes slightly less day information than central (-0.010).
  Sensitivity: seed-paired, without Feb 7 and without collapsed runs
  (none collapsed) are all EQUIVALENT. Fold 0 alone (3 days) is
  inconclusive, as expected from its width.
  The federated model's neighbourhood structure agrees with central as
  closely as two central runs agree with each other (15-NN overlap 0.696
  vs 0.699).

6.3 Confirmatory question 2: server pretraining -> HELPS (lobes)
  S+F vs F-nat  +0.049 [+0.034, +0.063]  p = 3e-6
  S+F vs F-X+P  +0.038 [+0.026, +0.050]  p = 7e-6
  Polarity +0.004 (not significant). The gain is not extra compute (it
  beats F-X+P). It is larger for federated than for central (interaction
  +0.038 [+0.021, +0.055]; S+C over C256 only +0.011). S+F vs C256:
  NON-INFERIOR, +0.026 on lobes.

6.4 Descriptive (no claims)
  - Non-IID clients cost nothing detectable: F-nat vs F-IID -0.008 (EQ).
  - Central with batch 64 is above central with batch 256 (+0.035). F-IID
    vs C64: -0.049 (INCONCLUSIVE, partition-dependent). The federated
    shortfall relative to batch-matched central appears to come from
    averaging, not from non-IID data.
  - Ablations: optimiser reset each round, or 50 rounds instead of 200,
    change nothing within the margin (report 21).
  - The 434 server events alone already give a model close to central
    (S0: lobes -0.045).
  - No model collapsed. All gates passed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. "Equivalent" means within +-0.04 (lobes) and +-0.02 (polarity) of the
     reference. Federated is measurably lower (by 0.023 and 0.010); the
     equivalence does not mean "identical".
  2. The reference was C256. Its learning rate (1.5e-4) and the other tuned
     values came from one seed on one day, a known noise source. C64, a
     central variant, was better on lobes. Against it, federated is about
     one margin lower (descriptive). A study aiming at "federated vs the
     best possible central" should tune all central variants on more data.
  3. The targets are morphology-derived shape descriptors (circular with
     respect to morphology). The claims are about waveform-shape
     information, not about vehicles. There are no vehicle labels.
  4. The results are conditional on the 3 day-based partitions. The day
     stratum treats the 3 days of a fold as independent, so with an
     arm x fold interaction the SE is somewhat understated. The
     partition-dependence flag is the safeguard; it was not raised for the
     confirmatory contrasts.
  5. Single sensor, single site, winter, SNR >= 20, 434 server events. All
     days had been used in earlier phases, so the study is exploratory with
     respect to the dataset, even though every evaluation day was unseen by
     its models.
  6. The nuisance gate measures day information in the representation; it
     is not a privacy guarantee.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. CLAIMS (as allowed by the pre-registered list, report 09 section 9)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - On these 9 unseen days and these 3 partitions, FedAvg across 10
    day-clients gives representations within +-0.04 (lobes) and +-0.02
    (polarity) of central training, measured by morphology-derived probes,
    without more day-nuisance encoding than central.
  - Pretraining on disjoint server days helps FedAvg on lobe structure,
    beyond the effect of the extra compute. With it, the federated model is
    non-inferior to central training.
  Not claimed:
    - "federated is as good as central" in general;
    - anything about vehicle types;
    - other partitions, client counts, sensors or sites.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. For a deployable federated system: use a server-pretrained model as
     the starting point (the clearest gain found).
  2. To test "federated vs the best central": a follow-up with central
     batch-size and learning-rate tuning over several seeds and days, then
     a new frozen comparison on fresh data.
  3. Fresh, unused recordings would remove the "exploratory" status.
  4. Any vehicle-level claim needs external labels, or a validated physics
     simulator (report 05).
