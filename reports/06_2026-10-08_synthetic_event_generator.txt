SUMMARY
-------
A cheap, data-driven generator that imitates the SNR >= 20 vehicle events of
the dataset, family by family. It produces events in exactly the format the
encoder sees: 12 s crops, 50 Hz, 600 samples, baseline-subtracted counts,
centred on the event (max-normalise them before feeding the encoder). Every
synthetic event comes with known generation parameters: shape family,
polarity, stretch, shift, amplitude, noise level and SNR.

Cost: CPU only (numpy/scipy). About 4 s of fitting plus 10 s of data loading;
about 20 000 events per second when sampling; full validation about 1 min.
The model file is 1.2 MB.

Congruence with real held-out days (4 test days, 1 412 real events):
  - morph-feature distributions match as closely as two sets of REAL days
    match each other (median KS 0.026 vs 0.024);
  - family identity is preserved (0.78 of synthetic events are recognised as
    their own family, vs 0.80 for real events);
  - SNR distribution matches (KS 0.044);
  - no copying of training events (nearest-neighbour distance ratio 0.94;
    5.5 % below the real 5th percentile, 5 % expected);
  - BUT a classifier can still tell synthetic from real somewhat: AUC 0.56
    (morph features), 0.60 (encoder embedding), 0.90 (log spectrum), against
    0.51 / 0.51 / 0.79 for real-vs-real. The generator is congruent in
    distribution, not indistinguishable.

Use it for controlled, RELATIVE experiments (planted controls, augmentation
checks, data-scaling and non-IID federated scenarios). Do not use it to
measure absolute accuracy that is then claimed for real data.

1. DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Unit: the stratum
  The 7 shape families of C2 (research/model/C2_lookalikes/families.csv;
  Louvain on aligned waveform correlation >= 0.9; 87.7 % of SNR >= 20
  events) are split by polarity. Families 1, 2 and 4 mix both signs, and
  averaging a + and a - shape gives nonsense. Strata with fewer than 30
  training events are dropped. 10 strata remain:

    family  polarity  n_train  PCA modes  variance kept  template fit (corr)
    0       -        511      18         0.954          0.972
    1       -        417      17         0.951          0.961
    1       +         84       9         0.956          0.965
    2       -        138      17         0.954          0.977
    2       +        289      17         0.955          0.968
    3       -        425      20         0.867          0.973
    4       -         42       9         0.961          0.939
    4       +        254      12         0.952          0.954
    5       +        108      20         0.937          0.934
    6       -         31       8         0.959          0.955
  Template fit = median correlation between each event and its warped
  template.

1.2 Shape model (per stratum)
  1. Low-pass each real crop at 5 Hz (zero-phase) and divide by its peak.
  2. Register every event to a template by a grid search over stretch
     (0.6-1.7, 31 values) and shift (+-1 s), maximising correlation. The
     template is refined 4 times (median of registered events), and its
     gauge is fixed so that it represents the TYPICAL event (geometric-mean
     stretch, median shift).
  3. PCA on the registered shapes; keep 95 % of the variance, at most
     20 modes.
  So each real event becomes a parameter vector:
     [PCA coefficients, log stretch, shift, log amplitude, log noise sigma].

1.3 Sampling: smoothed bootstrap
  Pick a real training event's vector at random and add Gaussian jitter
  with covariance bw^2 x (stratum covariance). This keeps the real joint
  dependencies (e.g. between amplitude, duration and noise floor) and does
  not assume Gaussian marginals. bw = 0.2 (chosen on the validation day,
  section 2.2).

1.4 Rendering
  shape  = template mean + PCA modes, peak-normalised
  clean  = amplitude x warp(shape, stretch, shift), then the extraction's own
           8 Hz 4th-order low-pass (the warping adds content above it)
  noise  = a random 12 s stretch of REAL background from the noise pool
           (ssl_central/bgpool.py, training days only), passed through a
           31-tap colouring filter, times the sampled noise sigma
  event  = clean + noise

  The colouring filter is fitted on training days. It maps pool noise onto
  the spectrum of the quiet edges of real crops (short-event families only).
  It corrects three measured differences:
    - the pool is whiter than real background;
    - sigma_ev (21 s window) over-states the local noise;
    - the 50 Hz rendering of real crops (linear interpolation) damps high
      frequencies, and the pool was never rendered that way.
  Without the filter and the 8 Hz low-pass, synthetic events had about 500x
  too much power at 20 Hz.

1.5 Outputs of sample()
  x [n, 600] float32 counts, plus per event: family, polarity, stretch
  (relative to the family template; > 1 = longer), lag_s, amp (peak counts),
  sigma (noise counts), snr_peak (= amp / sigma).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. VALIDATION PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Splits (the M4/M5 split)
  fit         training days (2 636 events)
  bandwidth   validation day Feb 10 (327 events)
  K_MAX       4 development days carved from the training days (Feb 5, 9,
              14, 24); generator refitted without them
  test        Feb 7, 13, 19, 25 (1 412 events), scored by synth/validate.py

2.2 Selection
  bandwidth   C2ST AUC (embedding) on Feb 10:
                bw 0.1: 0.566 | 0.2: 0.542 | 0.3: 0.570 | 0.5: 0.579 |
                0.8: 0.649  -> 0.2
              Smaller bw moves toward copying; larger bw blurs shapes.
  K_MAX       dev-day AUC (embedding / morph / log PSD):
                12: 0.596 / 0.581 / 0.900
                20: 0.584 / 0.578 / 0.896
                30: 0.582 / 0.573 / 0.900   -> 20 (no gain beyond)

2.3 Tests (synthetic counts matched to the real test set per stratum)
  V1  classifier two-sample test (C2ST): gradient boosting, 5-fold
      out-of-fold AUC, balanced classes, in three spaces: 19 morph features
      (central 8 s), the M5 SimCLR-TF (seed 0) embedding, log Welch PSD
      0.4-13 Hz. 0.5 = indistinguishable.
  V2  KS distance per morph feature.
  V3  family fidelity: the stratum template that best matches each event
      (correlation after optimal stretch and shift) is from its family.
  V4  copying: distance in embedding space to the nearest TRAINING event,
      synthetic vs real test events.
  Every number is reported next to the same number for REAL training-day
  events vs the test days. That is the natural day-to-day difference, and
  the bar the generator should meet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  V1 C2ST AUC, morph8                0.562               0.513
  V1 C2ST AUC, embedding             0.597               0.510
  V1 C2ST AUC, log PSD               0.901               0.789
  V2 median KS, 19 morph features    0.026               0.024
  V3 family fidelity                 0.778               0.800 (real test)
  V4 NN-distance ratio (syn/test)    0.937               1 by definition
     fraction below test p5          0.055               0.05 expected
  SNR (peak/sigma) p10/p50/p90       16 / 74 / 227       17 / 80 / 265 (test)

  Per family, embedding C2ST AUC (synthetic vs test):
    family 0: 0.53   1: 0.57   2: 0.59   3: 0.64   4: 0.57   5: 0.62
    (family 6 too small to test). The long, weak families 3 and 5 are the
    hardest: their shapes are dominated by noise and slow wander.
  The per-family real-vs-real baselines (0.39-0.58) are noisy at these sample
  sizes (49-270 events); compare with 0.5.

  Morph features with the largest synthetic-vs-test KS: zc_rate, dur10,
  area_neg_n, t_spread (all <= 0.11 in the first version, all now close to
  the baseline; full table in results.json, "V2_ks_morph8").

  What gives synthetic events away in the spectrum: no single frequency
  (per-bin AUC within 0.5 +- 0.12). The classifier combines a slightly too
  steep fall-off at 10-13 Hz and a slightly smaller spread between events.
  Real noise is strongly day-specific (real-vs-real PSD AUC is already 0.79).

  Visual check: gallery_real_vs_synth.png (6 random events per family, real
  test days vs synthetic). By eye the two columns are hard to tell apart.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Not indistinguishable: classifiers reach AUC 0.60 (embedding) and 0.90
    (spectrum). Absolute scores measured on synthetic data do not transfer
    to real data.
  - Test days were not fully untouched. The first two validation runs on the
    test days revealed the spectral error, and the noise-colouring filter and
    the 8 Hz low-pass were added in response. Both are fixed by physics or by
    training-day data (no parameter was tuned on test days), but the decision
    to add them was test-informed. K_MAX and bw were chosen without test days.
  - The C2 families were defined on all days, including the test days.
  - Coverage: 12.3 % of SNR >= 20 events belong to no family and are not
    modelled; strata with < 30 training events are dropped (e.g. family 0
    positive polarity, 1 % of family 0).
  - SNR >= 20 only. Sub-threshold events, run-0204 dropout blips, pulser
    spikes and telegraph steps are not generated: the noise pool was built to
    exclude transients.
  - Slow baseline ramps and neighbouring vehicles inside the crop are not
    modelled (they appear in real events, e.g. family 5).
  - Parameters are STATISTICAL, not physical. "stretch" is relative to the
    family template and mixes speed and vehicle length; "amp" mixes mass and
    distance. A physics model (dipole, report 05 section 2.B) would be needed
    for speed or offset labels.
  - The smoothed bootstrap stays close to the training data. It interpolates
    between observed events; it cannot invent new vehicle types.
  - One embedding (M5 SimCLR-TF seed 0) is used for V1/V4. A generator that
    fools this encoder could still be detectable by another.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT IT IS GOOD FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Planted-control dry runs (report 04, T1): datasets with known families,
    known SNR and known stretch, to check that every pass/fail rule can pass
    and fail.
  - Augmentation checks (T3): does the encoder move along "stretch" or "amp"
    when only that parameter changes? Sample with everything fixed except one
    parameter.
  - Non-IID federated scenarios (report 05, 2.A, T12): give each simulated
    client a controlled family mix, SNR range or noise level, and measure how
    the federated model degrades as heterogeneity grows. Ground truth of the
    heterogeneity is known exactly.
  - Data-scaling and class-balance studies: generate more events of rare
    families (6, 5, 4-) and test whether balancing helps.
  - Anomaly detection (report 05, section 4): synthetic vehicles as inliers,
    against planted steps or spikes.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  .venv/bin/python -m synth.validate          # refit, choose bw, score, write
                                              # data/synth/model.npz (~1 min)
  .venv/bin/python -m synth fit [--bw 0.2] [--all-days]
  .venv/bin/python -m synth sample --n 5000 [--family 2 --polarity 1]
                                   [--seed 0] [--out path.npz]

  In Python:
    from synth.generator import Generator
    import os
    g = Generator.load("data/synth/model.npz")
    X, info = g.sample(1000, rng=0)                 # mixture of families
    X2, info2 = g.sample(200, key=(3, -1), rng=1)   # one stratum
    Xn = X / abs(X).max(1, keepdims=True)           # encoder input

  --all-days fits on every day (more data). Use it for production sampling
  only: no held-out days remain to validate it.

  A sample of 2 000 events is in data/synth/sample_2000_seed0.npz.
