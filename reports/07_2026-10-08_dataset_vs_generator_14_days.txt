SUMMARY
-------
Question: when the generator runs over the same 14 days (342 recorded hours)
as the real dataset, does it produce the same event density, the same
clusters in the same proportions, and the same waveforms, without inventing
new clusters?

Answer: yes, within the families it models.
  - Density in time: daily counts correlate 0.975 with the real ones, and the
    weekday hourly profile 0.999 (morning peak at 09 h, second hump at
    13-14 h).
  - Cluster concentration: the family proportions match the real ones
    (total variation distance 0.016; chi-square p = 0.27). The mix by time of
    day also matches, e.g. family 3 is about half of the night-time events in
    both.
  - Cluster type: 93.7 % of synthetic events are assigned back to the family
    they were generated from, using the same correlation rule as the real
    clusters.
  - No new clusters: re-clustering real and synthetic events TOGETHER gives
    communities that are all about half real, half synthetic (0.47-0.52 vs
    0.47 expected). None is synthetic-only, and none is real-only.
  - Waveforms: per-family median shapes correlate 0.994-0.9999 with the real
    ones, and the within-family spread is the same (e.g. 0.952 vs 0.954).
  - Feature densities: amplitude, duration, lobe count, SNR and noise level
    match (KS 0.009-0.021). A classifier separates synthetic from real only
    weakly (AUC 0.52 on morph features, 0.54 on the encoder embedding).
These figures are for the CROSS-FITTED run, where every day is generated by a
model that never saw that day. The in-sample run is equally good.

Not imitated (by design): the 12.3 % of real events that belong to no
cluster. The generator also produces fewer "odd" events than the real tail
(section 4.4).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT WAS COMPARED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Real dataset: all 4 375 events with SNR >= 20 over the 4 runs (Feb 4-27,
342 recorded hours; 48 h of weekend). Of these, 3 838 (87.7 %) belong to one
of the 7 C2 shape families; 537 belong to none.

Synthetic "14-day run": the generator (report 06) plus a new timeline model
(synth/timeline.py), simulated over EXACTLY the recorded hours of the real
dataset (run start/end times from the dataset folder names):
  - arrival rate per hour of day, weekday vs weekend = real family events /
    recorded hours (an inhomogeneous Poisson process);
  - which family x polarity arrives = the real mix per 6-hour bin and
    daytype, smoothed toward the overall mix (Dirichlet, 10 events);
  - each event's waveform: the generator, conditioned on that stratum.
The generator can only draw the 10 family x polarity strata it was fitted
on. Asking for any other stratum raises an error (sample_keys). So it cannot
create a new family label. Whether its SHAPES could still form a new cluster
is tested in section 4.3.

Two synthetic datasets:
  insample  generator + timeline fitted on all days: the production model.
  crossfit  calendar days split into two alternating folds; each fold is
            generated by a model fitted on the other fold, with noise taken
            only from the other fold's days. This is the honest test: no
            synthetic event comes from a model that saw its day. Strata need
            >= 15 events per fold (instead of 30), so family 6 is kept.

Cluster rule (identical for real and synthetic events): the C2 definition.
Aligned correlation = maximum Pearson correlation over shifts of +-1 s
between raw 12 s crops; two events are linked if it is >= 0.9. The GPU
implementation reproduces the C2 correlation matrix to 1.4e-6.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. NO NEW CLUSTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 By construction
  Only the 10 fitted strata can be drawn (section 1).

4.2 Joint re-clustering (the decisive test)
  The C2 procedure (graph at corr >= 0.9, Louvain, communities >= 20 events)
  was run on real + synthetic events TOGETHER (8 271 events in crossfit). If
  the generator had invented a shape type, it would appear as a community
  made almost only of synthetic events. If it missed one, as an almost
  all-real community.

    crossfit community  size  synthetic share  main family (share)
    0                   1689  0.499            0 (0.97)
    1                   1670  0.516            1 (0.97)
    2                   1481  0.498            2 (0.95)
    3                   1402  0.486            3 (0.94)
    4                   1002  0.502            4 (0.99)
    5                    460  0.491            5 (0.74) + family 6
    expected synthetic share 0.471
    synthetic-only communities (>= 90 % synthetic): 0
    real-only communities (>= 90 % real): 0

  In-sample: 7 communities, one per family, synthetic share 0.42-0.51;
  again 0 synthetic-only and 0 real-only.
  In the crossfit graph, Louvain merged the two smallest families (5 and 6)
  into one community. This is a resolution effect of Louvain on that graph,
  not a new cluster: the merged community is half real, half synthetic, like
  all the others.

4.3 Distance to the nearest real event (novelty)
    best correlation with a real event     p10     median   no neighbour >= 0.9
    real family members (to other reals)   0.961   0.99     0 %
    synthetic events                       0.970   0.994    1.1 %
  Synthetic events sit as close to the real data as real family members sit
  to each other. 1.1 % have no real neighbour at 0.9. They are scattered (no
  community forms from them, 4.2): edge-of-family variants, not a new type.

4.4 The other side: the generator is a little too "typical"
  Real data contains odd events (the 12.3 % in no family; real "isolated"
  9.7 %), and some families have real tails the generator does not reproduce:
  the slow baseline ramps visible in family 5's upper band after 8 s
  (crossfit_waveforms.png). The smoothed bootstrap interpolates between
  observed events, so it under-represents rare oddities. For the purpose
  "same clusters, no new clusters" this is the right side to err on.

  The 2-D projection of the encoder embedding has the same high-density

  spot on the right), and each family occupies the same region in both.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Only the 87.7 % of events that belong to a family are imitated. A
    synthetic 14-day run has about 3 870 events where the real data has
    4 375 (SNR >= 20).
  - The timeline uses one weekday profile and one weekend profile. It does
    not know day-specific effects (holidays, a quiet Friday) and has only one
    real weekend to learn from.
  - Rare real oddities and slow baseline ramps are under-represented
    (4.4).
  - The clusters themselves are post-hoc and exploratory (C2), and were
    built from all days, including the days each crossfit fold generates.
  - The joint re-clustering uses one Louvain seed. The merge of families 5
    and 6 in crossfit shows that the smallest families sit at the limit of
    Louvain's resolution.
