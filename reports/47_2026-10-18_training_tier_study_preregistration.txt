1. QUESTION
  A deployment will see clean and noisy events at every site. At FIXED
  compute (the study's budgets), should sites train on:
    T20  clean events only (SNR >= 20; 4 375 events): the study's runs;
    T10  all events (SNR >= 10; 9 659 events): NEW runs; or
    TLO  noisy events only (10 <= SNR < 20): the replication runs?
  The answer becomes the data rule in the runbook.

2. DESIGN
  - Arms: C256 (central), F-nat (federated), S+F (server pretraining +
    federated, the deployment recipe).
  - T10 runs: study commands verbatim (rep4_manifest.json; lrs: C256
    1.5e-4, F-nat 3e-4, S+F 1e-3). Servers retrained on T10 server-day
    events (911). 10 seeds x 3 folds per arm. 100 runs.
  - T20 = the study's runs v21_*; TLO = the replication runs rep10_*.
    Neither is retrained.
  - Same folds, clients, noise pool, augmentations, budgets. T10 clients
    have 194-1 005 events.
  - ALL models are scored with RELIABLE labels: the study's frozen
    evaluator on the SNR >= 20 tier (probes on each fold's clean client
    events, clean unseen evaluation days). Gate (nuisance encoding) for
    S+F T10 and T20 with the study's m_leak 0.03.
  Note on fairness: the T20 and T10 models trained on the very client
  events the probes are fitted on. TLO models did not. This is the
  study's protocol, it is the same for T20 and T10 (the primary
  comparison), and the evaluation days are unseen by all.

3. PRE-REGISTERED DECISION (rep4.py analyze)
  PRIMARY: S+F trained on T10 vs S+F trained on T20 (two-stratum t,
  90 % CI, study margins), plus the nuisance gate (S+F|T10 vs S+F|T20).
    - Gain: label SUPERIOR, or EQ/NI with the lobes CI lower bound > 0;
      and gate PASS -> RULE "USE ALL EVENTS (SNR >= 10)".
    - EQUIVALENT or NON-INFERIOR without a gain:
        gate PASS -> "NO FILTER NEEDED";
        gate FAIL -> "FILTER TO SNR >= 20".
    - INFERIOR, or the lobes CI entirely < 0 -> "FILTER TO SNR >= 20".
    - Otherwise -> "UNDECIDED".
  SECONDARY (reported): the same T10 vs T20, TLO vs T20 and T10 vs TLO
    contrasts for C256 and F-nat, and TLO contrasts for S+F; mean scores.
