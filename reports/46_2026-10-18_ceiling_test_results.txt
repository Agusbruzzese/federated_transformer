SUMMARY
-------
PRIMARY: HELPS -> READING B. Server pretraining DOES help low-SNR clients.
The gain was hidden by the noisy low-SNR labels.
Scored with reliable (clean-tier) labels, the report-42 arm S+F beats
plain federated by +0.029 on lobes and beats equal-compute federated by
+0.023. On noisy labels the same models had shown -0.020.
The two other pretrained variants point the same way (+0.025 and +0.015
over plain federated, both significant). Their labels are "DOES NOT HELP"
only because one of their two gains is below the half-margin threshold
(0.02).
The procedure was valid (C1 equivalent, C3 inferior).

2. CEILING MEASURES
  Untrained -> central gap on lobes: 0.26 with clean labels vs 0.10 with
  low-SNR labels (report 44). The low-SNR labels compressed the
  measurable range by about 2.6x, which hid differences of 0.02-0.03.
  The spread of trained arms is still small (0.029). The models
  themselves, trained on noisy data, are close to each other.

3. WHAT THIS SETTLES (answers "why did the gain disappear?")
  - The gain did not disappear; the measurement lost it. With low-SNR
    probe labels the evaluator cannot resolve differences of 0.02-0.03
    (reading B).
  - With reliable labels, pretraining helps low-SNR clients on lobes
    (+0.015 to +0.029 depending on the variant). That is smaller than on
    clean clients (+0.049), but it is real.
  - Reports 42 and 44 should be read with this correction:
      - their low-SNR-label conclusions on QS (no gain) and QF (federated
        better) are artifacts of label noise;
      - with clean labels: QF EQUIVALENT, QS HELPS. Both match the
        study.
  - Server data quality: the clean server alone (0.555) is the best
    model of all. Federated training on noisy clients does not improve
    it (Sclean+F 0.547). Noisy client data add little beyond a good
    starting point.
  - Learning rate: on clean labels the original 1e-3 is better than the
    re-tuned 3e-4 (+0.029 vs +0.015 over plain federated). The tuning-day
    signal on noisy labels (report 44) was itself misleading. Keep 1e-3.

4. LESSONS FOR METHOD
  - Evaluate with reliable labels. Derived labels from noisy waveforms
    can compress or invert real differences.
  - When the untrained-to-trained gap shrinks strongly, suspect the
    measurement before the method.

5. LIMITS
  Same days and same sensor. The clean-tier labels are the study's own
  evaluation data (used before for other models, never for these). The
  primary is one pre-registered comparison; the secondaries agree in
  direction but not by the full rule.
