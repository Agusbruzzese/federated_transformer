# External audit: research/extraction/B_signal

_20260929_221129 · served by claude-sonnet-5-5 · effort medium · 48043 in / 4288 out · ~$0.139_

## Verdict
**ACCEPT WITH FIXES**: the characterisation is broadly thorough, but several headline numbers rest on a biased sample, a mains-based clock assumption, and inconsistent internal figures. The recommended chain also contradicts what the scripts implement.

## Issues

1. [MAJOR] REPORT §2/§3.4 vs scripts/fig_events.py: `ev = ev[~ev.edge]` drops only events whose start is exactly at index 0 or whose end is at the chunk end. The orchestrator caveat says edge events bias duration/extent statistics, but the reported tables (S6, §3.4) come from this filter. Events truncated within 1 s of an edge are not handled, and `e >= len(z)-1` is a 100 Hz-grid comparison. The 8-σ core plus 2-σ extension is also affected by the running-median edge effect (`mode="nearest"`). -> Exclude events within ~5 s (median-window plus event extent) of a chunk edge or dropout, and recompute the tables. Alternatively, recompute durations from the contiguous windows.

2. [MAJOR] S1/§3.1: "ADC rate = 2416 Hz" is derived by assuming mains = exactly 50 Hz, and the ±2.8 Hz spread is then read as grid variation. This is circular for any use of the mains line as a clock. The wall-clock gives 2413–2414 Hz for runs 1–3, a 2–3 Hz discrepancy. The alternative "fs = 2400 Hz per Hardware.cfg" is dismissed without a test. The claim is stated as "high" confidence, and the 2.8 Hz spread is quoted as if it were uncertainty. -> Report the discrepancy against the wall-clock and the cfg, and state that the absolute fs is ±~0.1 %. Downstream time-scale numbers (durations, rates) inherit a ≈0.7 % uncertainty.

3. [MAJOR] S8/S9/§3.4 rates: hourly rates come from a 1-in-8 sample. This gives roughly 2–4 chunks (about 2 min) per hour-of-day per day, across 18 days (the report says "all 18 days" although the brief says 14). Values such as "57/h at 09 h" and "0 at night" carry no uncertainty. Only the 22 h of contiguous data are unaffected. A Saturday window with zero large events is one sample of a single day. -> Add Poisson or bootstrap-by-day CIs, and state the number of independent days per hour. Do not present weekday/weekend contrasts from n = 1.

4. [MAJOR] Circular detection and bimodality claims (S8, §4.7): the 8σ trigger and the 5000-count "large" split are fixed before looking at the event statistics. Then "gap at 3–5k" and "the small group is probably far-lane traffic or non-vehicle" are drawn from detections that pass the same threshold. The log-histogram shows a valley, but the small group is truncated at 8σ, so the "bimodality" is partly a detection-threshold artefact. The class "vehicle-like" is defined by duration ≥0.4 s, then p5 = 0.48 s is cited as proof that blips are separated by duration alone. -> Rerun with a lower trigger (5σ) and check that the valley persists. Do not use a duration that was itself the class definition as evidence of separation. Confidence for S8 should be "medium".

5. [MAJOR] Classification of dropout-overlapping events: 4476 of 6824 detections (66 %) are excluded as "dropout-overlap", mostly in run 0204. Every morphology statistic therefore comes from the 605 clean events. This is a selection bias toward the less-disturbed runs (runs 1–3 dominate), and run 0204's rate is acknowledged as biased low (10 vs 14–21/h). Nothing shows that the morphology is the same in run 0204. -> Report per-run morphology, or quantify how many masked events are true vehicles (e.g., by repairing the mask ±0.15 s instead of discarding the whole event).

6. [MAJOR] S6/§3.3 bandwidth and the LP recommendation: event bandwidth is measured from a series that has already been 20 Hz-LP'd and processed by the 5 Hz Butterworth detector, and the baseline was removed with a linear flank fit. "99 % of energy below 2.4 Hz" therefore holds only for large events; for all vehicle-like events, `bw99z` p75/p95 are 12/18 Hz, which the summary omits. Yet the 5 Hz cutoff is justified only on "large events". Small and fast events (the ones relevant to contrastive learning of vehicle types) are not tested. In `cutoff_test.py`, the peak ratio compares against the 20 Hz series, not the raw one, and uses `e.a-300` indices whose `e.a` refer to the chunk-relative grid. -> State that bandwidth conclusions hold for the >5k group only; report the medium/small group's `peak_ratio` from `cutoff_test.py` too.

7. [MAJOR] Preproc inconsistency: the REPORT recommends a 5 Hz LP, 50 Hz decimation with `resample_poly(x,25,1208)`, and a 20 s running median. `preproc.py` defaults to `lp_hz=10`, decimates by `round(2416/50)=48` (i.e., actual 50.33 Hz), takes every k-th sample after a 10 Hz LP, and the detector (`events.py`) uses a 15 s median, not 20 s. The 25/1208 ratio equals 2416/1208 → 2 Hz, i.e. 1208·2 = 2416 gives 2416·25/1208 = 50.0 Hz, which is fine only if the segment length is handled. Nothing validates the 20 s vs 15 s choices. Also, the report states "Noise drops from 115 to 85 counts" at 5 Hz; `chunk_stats` measured 99 (median). -> Align the code with the recommendation, or update the report. Test the recommended chain end-to-end on the same events.

8. [MAJOR] Mains regression (`remove_mains`, `chunk_stats.one`): the f0 estimate is taken from the longest segment, and a fixed f0 is applied over 10-s blocks. Harmonics at 20 × f0 = 1000 Hz then include the ADC-digital-filter roll-off region and the 604 Hz fs/4 artefact. A cubic trend is fitted jointly with the harmonics and is kept in `y`. The report itself notes the residual bursts of ±1000 counts. The fitted harmonics can partly absorb the 0.73/1.98/9.45 Hz tones only if they are at f0 multiples (they are not), so this is fine. However, the cubic trend is removed only for the 1..20 harmonic amplitudes and then dropped (`blk - H@co[4:]` retains the trend). This is consistent, but the 24160-sample blocks (10 s) in `chunk_stats` are not cross-checked against preproc's `block_s`. Discontinuities at block edges are not evaluated. -> Show that block-edge steps do not create the 0.2 s blips. A residual-mains spectrum check would suffice.

9. [MINOR] S2/S11: "DC-relative normalisation is not justified" rests on the code varying by ±1.6 %. This is only an argument about the effect size of division, not about sensor sensitivity dependence on bias (acknowledged unknown). Confidence "medium" for "unnecessary" is fine, but the summary row "high (subtract)" is stronger than the argument: subtracting is needed because of the slow ramps, and the running median subtraction will itself distort long/slow events (a 20 s window vs 6.6 s p95; queueing events >8 s are affected). -> Quantify the peak/area distortion of the 20 s median on synthetic or real large events (the report states 99.8 % peak retention only for the LP).

10. [MINOR] S10 "94 % of blips are in dropout chunks" and "rate grows 0.09→1.3 per chunk": correlation only. The mechanism is unproven, and "logger blips" may also be genuine small disturbances. Confidence "high" for (a–c) is fine for existence, but the blip = "logger artefact" label is an inference. Spike statistics come from an "inline check … script not saved", which is not reproducible.

11. [MINOR] Reproducibility: `preproc.py`, `fig_gallery.py` and the `events_lp100` output depend on `../data/lp100.npz` (git-ignored, 57 MB). `io_utils.ROOT` is hard-coded to an absolute path. Some scripts (`fig_psd.py`) read `data/psd.npz`, which is not listed among the outputs (only `psd_summary.npz`). The `quicklook.py` script still uses the discarded 2381 Hz and µs ticks. `gap_phase_check.py` samples with `rng.choice(C, 60)` on dicts (fine, seeded), but the check is described as covering "232 gaps" without recording how many were excluded by the `g<1500` filter. `fig_gallery.py` uses `rng = 7` for sampling, so the gallery is a random, not representative, pick.  The DC "day" list mentions 18 days but the brief says 14. -> Save all inline checks, list required inputs, and remove or mark the obsolete scripts.

12. [MINOR] S3 numbers: text says "33 counts/√Hz at 1–5 Hz" and "1126 counts²/Hz at 1 Hz", whereas `out_psd.txt` gives 963 → 31.0 counts/√Hz. The slope is quoted as −0.44 / −0.39 in the report, while `out_psd.txt` prints −0.335 / −0.403. The "1000 Hz" floor of 1 count²/Hz also depends on the ADC filter. The event-chunk PSD in S6 ("99 % of excess power below 1.4 Hz") is a ratio of medians, not per-event. -> Regenerate the text from the saved outputs.

13. [MINOR] S9 overlap rate: "Only 1–3 pairs per window <5 s apart" uses the start-to-start spacing of detections. This does not count overlaps that were merged by the 1 s merge rule, and the merged events (>8 s) were listed as 0–8 per window. This means the "overlaps are rare (2–5 %)" is a lower bound, and a large-event pair is probably not resolved at 8σ. Confidence "medium-high" is generous. -> Cite it as a lower bound.

14. [MINOR] Direction-of-travel (S7): the +−/−+ pattern percentages depend on the sign convention after the running-median subtraction and on the per-lobe 20 % threshold. Also 66 % negative dominant polarity, with no test of alternative explanations (which lane, sensor orientation). Fine as "untested", but "high (shapes)" is overconfident given that sign conventions are untested.
