ABSTRACT
--------
We trained small 1-D encoders (~208k parameters) with self-supervised
objectives on 4 375 vehicle events of high signal-to-noise ratio (SNR >= 20),
extracted from 340 h of a single tunnel-magneto-resistance (TMR) sensor
(see reports/01_2026-10-04_extraction_report_v2.txt). Each event is a
12 s, 50 Hz, max-normalised waveform of 600 samples. The encoder maps it to
a 64-d embedding.

Two architectures were compared: a GroupNorm CNN and a patch transformer.
Three objectives were compared: SimCLR/NT-Xent, VICReg and a masked
autoencoder (MAE). Training ran in two pre-registered phases:
  F  : 100 epochs (~1 200 steps), validation-selected hyper-parameters,
       evaluated on a held-out recording run.
  M5 : 10 000 steps with warm-up, 5 seeds per method, compared against
       untrained-network, raw and PCA baselines, evaluated on 4 held-out
       days.

Because no vehicle labels exist, representation quality was measured by
frozen-embedding probes (kNN and linear) predicting three physical shape
descriptors: polarity, lobe count and log duration.

Main findings:
  (i)   SimCLR-trained encoders encode lobe count clearly better than their
        own untrained weights (+0.07 to +0.17 skill; every 95 % CI
        excludes 0).
  (ii)  On polarity and duration an untrained CNN is already near the SSL
        level. The ceiling is near saturation.
  (iii) With 10k steps the transformer matches the CNN on lobes and is best
        on duration under a linear probe.
  (iv)  MAE optimised but produced the weakest pooled embedding.
  (v)   No representation identifies the problematic run 0204 above chance
        on held-out days.
  (vi)  Every encoder remains below the 19 hand-crafted morphology features
        on these (morphology-derived, hence circular) targets.

Under the strict pre-registered decision rule, no SSL method beat every
baseline. An independent review showed that rule had about 5 % power for a
+0.05 gain. A re-verification on 2026-10-04 confirmed that all checkpoints
load, are deterministic, are not collapsed, and reproduce the reported
behaviour.

Status: every result is EXPLORATORY. The targets are circular with respect
to morphology, there are no external labels, and in M5 the test days had
already been seen in an earlier phase (M4).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Goal: learn, without labels, an event representation that can later be
trained federatedly (one client per site, day or time slot). It should:
  - encode the physical shape of the vehicle's magnetic signature (lobe
    structure, polarity, duration);
  - NOT encode nuisance identity (date, recording run, dropout artefacts),
    which would make clients separable and harm federated generalisation;
  - provide a central benchmark against which the federated model can be
    compared.

Why self-supervision: the dataset is unlabeled (14 days, ~15k detected
events). Earlier repo work used KMeans pseudo-labels on EMD/HHT features.
That approach was discarded because of an int16 read bug, unstable EMD and
date leakage (see reports/01_2026-10-04_extraction_report_v2.txt,
section 9).

Research questions (from the pre-registrations):
  Q1  Does SSL encode event shape better than untrained networks and raw
      inputs?
  Q2  Which architecture and objective work best?
  Q3  Do the embeddings carry recording-run identity beyond what physical
      features carry?


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source: data/events/ (extraction pipeline output). Each event has a 1050-sample
baseline-subtracted window (~21 s at fs_eff 49.82-50.11 Hz), an event mask
[onset, offset], a repair mask, and 19 morphology scalars on the central ~8 s
(m8_*).

Tier: SNR >= 20, giving 4 375 events. Lower tiers are dominated by
non-vehicle detections and dropout blips from run 0204, which leak the run
identity (run skill 0.30 at all-SNR).

Splits:
  Phase F (PREREG_central v2):
      train      runs 0204, 0212, 0224 minus Feb 26          3 172 events
      validation Feb 26 (hyper-parameter selection only)       366
      holdout    run 0218, Feb 18-20 ("weakly held out")       837
                 (93 thirty-minute blocks)
  Phase M5 (same split as M4):
      train      all remaining days                          2 636
      validation Feb 10                                         327
      test       Feb 7, 13, 19, 25                            1 412
                 (167 thirty-minute blocks, 4 day-clusters)

  A firewall (ssl_central/data.firewall) asserts, on event_id sets, that no
  holdout or validation event enters training or selection. The sha256 of
  the sorted training IDs is logged in every checkpoint's log.json.

Encoder input (implementation choices I2/I3):
  - The 1050-sample window is linearly interpolated onto an exact 50 Hz grid
    (ratio fs_eff/50 differs from 1 by <= 0.23 %). A 600-sample (12 s) crop
    is taken.
  - Each view is divided by its own max|x| as the LAST step. The encoder
    therefore sees SHAPE only; absolute amplitude is withheld by design.
  - Evaluation view: deterministic crop centred on the detector's energy
    centroid (window index 524.5), no stretch.

Background-noise pool (ssl_central/bgpool.py), used for augmentation:
  - 12.8 s stretches (640 samples) of the same feature path, taken from
    TRAINING days only.
  - A stretch is accepted only if no detection of any SNR lies within 3 s,
    it has no dropout flag, and max|x - median| <= 6 robust sigma.
  - Stored at unit robust sigma: 1 930 stretches. M5 excludes the
    test/validation days from the pool.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ARCHITECTURES  (ssl_central/models.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Neither architecture uses BatchNorm. This avoids batch-statistics leakage in
contrastive training and suits a later federated setting with small,
non-IID client batches.

3.1 1-D CNN  (210 592 parameters)
   5 stages of Conv1d -> GroupNorm(8 groups) -> GELU
       channels     32, 64, 96, 128, 128
       kernel        9,  7,  7,   5,   5
       stride        2,  2,  2,   2,   1      (600 -> 38 time steps)
   global average pooling over time -> Linear(128 -> 64)  = embedding h

3.2 Patch transformer  (207 424 parameters, -1.5 % vs the CNN)
   patching   600 samples -> 24 non-overlapping patches of 25 samples (0.5 s)
   embedding  Linear(25 -> 64) + learnable positional embedding (24 x 64)
   encoder    4 pre-LayerNorm Transformer layers, d_model 64, 4 heads,
              FFN 256, GELU, dropout 0
   pooling    final LayerNorm, mean over tokens -> Linear(64 -> 64) = h

3.3 Heads (discarded after training; the 64-d h is what is evaluated)
   SimCLR projection head : Linear(64,64) -> ReLU -> Linear(64,64)
                            (8 320 parameters)
   VICReg expander        : Linear(64,256) -> ReLU -> Linear(256,256)
   MAE decoder (M5)       : Linear(64,128) -> GELU -> Linear(128,25), plus a
                            learnable [MASK] token (std 0.02 init)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SELF-SUPERVISED OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 SimCLR / NT-Xent (Chen et al., 2020) -- ssl_central/models.nt_xent
   For a batch of B events, two augmented views give projections z1, z2.
   After L2-normalisation, z = [z1; z2] (2B x 64) and the similarity matrix
   is S = z z^T / T. For each anchor i, its positive is the other view of the
   same event. The loss is cross-entropy over the row, excluding self:

      L = -(1/2B) * sum_i log( exp(S_i,pos(i)) / sum_{k != i} exp(S_i,k) )

   FALSE-NEGATIVE GUARD (project-specific): two different events whose
   centroids are less than 30 s apart are removed from each other's
   negatives (all four view combinations). Consecutive vehicles in a
   platoon, or one vehicle split in two by the detector, would otherwise be
   pushed apart. The fraction masked is logged; it is ~1e-3 to 1e-2.
   Temperature T: 0.1 or 0.2 (grid in F); 0.1 in M5.

4.2 VICReg (Bardes et al., 2022) -- M4_methods/m4.vicreg_loss
      L = 25 * MSE(z1, z2)                                    invariance
        + 25 * [mean ReLU(1 - sqrt(Var(z1) + 1e-4)) + same for z2]  variance
        +  1 * [sum of squared off-diagonal cov(z1) / d + same for z2]
                                                              covariance
   No negatives. Collapse is prevented by the variance and covariance terms.

4.3 Masked autoencoder (He et al., 2022, standard recipe) -- M5 MAE5
   - 75 % of the 24 patch tokens are replaced by the [MASK] token. Positions
     are added and the whole sequence is encoded (BERT-style; no token
     dropping).
   - The decoder predicts each masked patch. Targets are per-patch
     normalised (zero mean, unit SD), and the MSE is computed on masked
     patches only.
   - Input: random crop only (no other augmentation).
   - Embedding: the pooled, normalised tokens.

4.4 Augmentations for SimCLR and VICReg views (ssl_central/augment.py), on
    the GPU, in the pre-registered order:
   1. time-stretch s ~ U(0.85, 1.18) together with a random 12 s crop. The
      crop must contain the detector's [onset, offset] when it fits;
      otherwise the event centre must lie in the middle half of the crop.
      The main lobe is never cut away.
   2. amplitude scale ~ U(0.8, 1.25). It comes BEFORE noise, so it changes
      the event-to-noise ratio.
   3. additive REAL background noise from the pool, scaled by
      alpha * sigma_ev with alpha ~ U(0, 1). sigma_ev is the event's own
      robust noise floor, measured outside its mask (+-1 s). This is
      "SNR-matched" noise: the view's floor stays within sqrt(2) of the
      original.
   4. DC offset ~ U(-0.05, 0.05)*A plus end-to-end linear drift
      ~ U(-0.05, 0.05)*A, where A = crop max|x|. This mimics residual
      baseline wander.
   5. max-normalisation.
   FORBIDDEN, and therefore not implemented, because they change physics:
     sign flip (polarity is physical: direction or lane), time reversal
     (rise/fall asymmetry is physical), and masking below 4 Hz (vehicle
     energy is below 2.5 Hz).

6. EVALUATION METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Frozen encoders; the evaluation view (deterministic central crop) is
embedded.

Targets (from morphology on the central ~8 s; see extraction report):
   polarity   sign of the largest excursion (2 classes)
   lobes      number of alternating-sign lobes, capped at 4 (4 classes)
   duration   log10(m8_dur10), duration above 10 % of peak (regression)
   (F also used log_amp. That turned out to be SNR leakage through the noise
    floor of a max-normalised view, and was dropped from later phases.)

Metrics:
   classification: chance-corrected balanced accuracy,
                   skill = (BA - 1/K) / (1 - 1/K)
   regression:     R^2

Probes (fitted on training-split embeddings, scored on test):
   kNN    k = 15, Euclidean, uniform weights, z-scored
   linear LogisticRegression(C=1, max_iter=3000) for classes;
          RidgeCV(alphas = logspace(-3, 3, 13)) for duration
   Scaling (z-scored vs raw) chosen per representation on the VALIDATION day.

Baselines (M5):
   B1  untrained CNN (the init.pt of the same seeds as SimCLR-CNN)
   B2  untrained transformer, unpooled tokens (24x64 = 1 536-d) and PCA-64
   B3  raw 600-sample crop, and PCA-64
   B4  PCA-64 of the crop
   Ref the 19 m8_* morphology features: a ceiling only, and circular,
       because the targets are computed from it

Statistics (M5):
   - Two-level hierarchical bootstrap: days with replacement, then 30-min
     blocks within each day; B = 10 000. Seeds are resampled independently.
   - Two-sided p-values with Holm correction over 66 tests per probe.
   - "X beats Y": under BOTH probes, mean gap >= 0.02, Holm p < 0.05 and a
     95 % CI excluding 0.
   - Q1 decision: an SSL method must beat EACH of B1-B4 on >= 2 of 3 scores
     and lose on none.

Leakage (Q3): conditional run information
   Delta = skill(is-0204 | [embedding PCA-19, morph8])
         - skill(is-0204 | morph8)
   Day-grouped CV with logistic regression. Flag if mean Delta > 0.05 and
   the CI's lower bound > 0.

Dry runs with planted controls (morph8 polarity >= 0.90, planted run column
Delta >= 0.50, morph8 copy Delta <= 0.05) had to pass before any training.

7.3 Independent review of M5 (M5_long/review/REVIEW.md), with recomputation
    from frozen checkpoints:
  - All numbers reproduce exactly.
  - Lobes: SimCLR (both backbones) is +0.07 to +0.17 over B1/B2 under both
    probes; all 8 CIs exclude 0. This is a real gain.
  - Power: the Q1 rule had about 1-5 % power against a uniform +0.05 gain
    and about 70 % against +0.10. B1 is near the ceiling (polarity 0.885,
    duration 0.82). "No evidence of value" is therefore partly a property of
    the rule.
  - Q2 "three-way tie" is a probe disagreement: under the linear probe,
    SimCLR-TF beats VICReg (+0.043) and SimCLR-CNN (+0.023) on duration on
    all 4 days.
  - Q3: the SimCLR-TF flag lies inside a day-permutation null (95th
    percentile 0.07-0.10). SimCLR-CNN's 0.127 exceeds the null (p ~ 0.016),
    meaning it changes how morph8 generalises across days. It does NOT mean
    run 0204 is decodable on unseen days.
  - A bug in verdict() (baseline variant matched without filtering by probe)
    affected 3 cells. Correcting it does not change Q1 or Q2. verdict.json
    should be regenerated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. DISCUSSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What the evidence supports (exploratory, SNR >= 20, morphology-derived
targets):
  1. Contrastive SSL learns lobe structure that random networks lack. This
     is the most robust effect across F, G, H2, M4 and M5.
  2. A random-init CNN is already a strong shape featuriser (an
     architectural inductive bias: local convolution plus pooling). SSL adds
     little to polarity or duration, and the CNN can even LOSE duration
     information during SimCLR (0.815 -> 0.771). A plausible cause is the
     time-stretch augmentation (0.85-1.18), which explicitly asks the
     encoder to be invariant to duration.
  3. The transformer gains most from SSL: untrained it is weak (effective
     rank ~10). With 10k steps it matches the CNN and is best on duration
     (linear probe). Because it is cleaner on leakage, it is the preferred
     candidate for the federated phase.
  4. VICReg is competitive and the cleanest on leakage. MAE trains but its
     pooled embedding is poor at this data scale. Only one MAE recipe was
     tried, so this does not mean masked modelling is worse in general.
  5. All learned and untrained networks greatly outperform raw and PCA
     inputs (linear lobes +0.38 to +0.46; duration R2 +0.65 to +0.72),
     because the representation is shift-tolerant.

Threats to validity:
  - Circularity: the targets are functions of morph8, so morph8 is
    trivially the ceiling. The scores measure shape descriptors, not vehicle
    class.
  - No labels: nothing links lobes or duration to vehicle type, axles or
    speed yet.
  - Re-use of test data: the M5 test days were seen in M4, and the F holdout
    was "weakly held out". There is no fresh test data; all 340 h have been
    used somewhere.
  - Few clusters: 4 test days mean any comparison must be positive on all 4
    days to pass Holm.
  - Single sensor, single site, 14 days, winter only.
  - The SNR >= 20 restriction (29 % of events) applies to every conclusion.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Recommended central model: M5 SimCLR patch transformer
    (data/m5/ckpt/simclr_tf_s{0..4}/final.pt; lr 3e-4, T 0.1, 10k steps).
    It improves on its init on every target, has no run-0204 leakage beyond
    the null, and its patch structure suits federated aggregation.
    SimCLR-CNN is a close second, but carries some extra run-0204
    information.
  - Use it as the benchmark for the federated phase. The existing client/
    and server/ code still uses the old EMD/KMeans pipeline and must be
    rewired to these events, this encoder and NT-Xent with global
    normalisation.
  - The most valuable next step is external labels (camera, traffic counter
    or axle log) for a few hundred events. With them, label-efficiency
    curves (10/30/100/300 labels) of SSL vs untrained networks can be
    measured on run-held-out splits. This removes the circularity and is the
    only route to claims about vehicles.
  - Alternatives: a fresh evaluation with >= 8 held-out days; reduce the
    stretch augmentation range to preserve duration; pre-train on all SNR
    tiers (about 3x more unlabeled data) with a blip filter; a transformer
    patch-size ablation (0.1-0.2 s patches).
