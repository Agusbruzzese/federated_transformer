  - Two independent groups of 10 central seeds differ by +0.003 on LOBES,
    with a 90 % CI half-width of about 0.011, well inside the 0.04 margin.
    The procedure has room to declare equivalence if federated training
    truly matches central.
  - A degradation of about 1x the lobe margin (C5, -0.041) is NOT called
    equivalent. A degradation of about 2.4x the margin (C4) is called
    INFERIOR.
  - The gate flags a planted day offset of 5 % of the embedding scale
    (kappa*). Smaller offsets (<= 3 %) are mostly undetectable by the
    per-seed-fold null, so the gate's resolution is about that size.
