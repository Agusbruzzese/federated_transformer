# External audit: research/extraction/C_detection

_20260930_022751 · served by claude-sonnet-5-5 · effort medium · 145086 in / 3330 out · ~$0.323_

## Verdict
**ACCEPT WITH FIXES**: The injection-based detector comparison is reasonably supported. The real-data precision claims are not: the labels are AI-only and not SNR-blind, and the clean-tier cut was fitted on 20 items. The 21 s window and the "high" confidence labels also need correcting.

## Issues

1. [MAJOR] REPORT §1/§6.2: The clean-tier precision of 0.96 [0.87, 0.99] rests on AI labels. The sheets showed SNR and duration in their titles (`postfilter.sheets` prints `dur`, and `handcheck.make` prints `peak … sigma`). The cut (SNR ≥ 30) was fitted on 20 passing items after the holdout raw precision of 0.40 had been seen. The 20 passing items are also the top of a continuous SNR distribution, so tuning-vs-holdout agreement is close to guaranteed. → Treat all precision numbers as provisional until the blind human pack is labelled, and keep them out of any recommendation.

2. [MAJOR] `human_pack.py` / README: The human pack is unlabelled, so the brief's hand-verified criterion is not met. Panels are auto-scaled per panel, so the waveform's visible noisiness still leaks SNR. The pack has 80 items (fewer than the pre-registered ≥200) and is Run-3-only, so it cannot support per-detector Wilson CIs. The BDET@8 items were re-drawn to avoid overlap with HYST-BP, so BDET's sample is conditioned on HYST-BP's draw. → Have a human label the pack before the two-tier recommendation is adopted. Add a fresh-day confirmation set.

3. [MAJOR] §6.1 / F1: The F1 metric is defined only over injections, where every injected item is a true positive by construction. Its precision term is 1 minus (fragments plus new detections), and surrogate FA is Gaussian and near zero for all detectors. F1 therefore mostly measures recall plus fragmentation. FIXEDLOC's F1 is penalised by 4 s window splitting, which is an artefact of the metric. The headline ΔF1 = +0.157 vs FIXEDLOC largely measures segmentation, not detection, as the report itself admits. → Report recall at matched real-data FP (AI-labelled or human) instead of F1, or present the F1 gap explicitly as a segmentation effect.

4. [MAJOR] §3 E4/E9: The recommended detector, HYST-BP, was added post hoc after tuning scores. It was then "chosen by the pre-registered decision rule" against competitors it was tuned relative to (hp_s = 4, pulse median). Its tie-break win over CUSUM-BP (the ΔF1 CI includes 0 for the independent templates) is the deciding factor. The confidence label of "[medium]" is fair, but the earlier claim of "[high]" for the detector persists in the docstring and other text. → Align the confidence wording everywhere. State that CUSUM-BP is statistically tied, and that HYST-BP was preferred on segmentation, not detection.

5. [MAJOR] §6.6 / `detector.py`: The 21 s default comes from a rule whose input (`x_dur`) is capped by ±10 s context, so it is a lower bound that partly reflects the context window. The p50 extent of all events (15 s) is dominated by noise energy. At 21 s, contamination by another clean event is about 26 %, and ramp-inclusive truncation stays at 22 % at 20 s. The 12 s alternative is dismissed on a criterion that itself depends on the arbitrary context. → Make the contamination-vs-truncation tradeoff explicit to D, and recompute the extent with a noise-floor cut (for example, energy above 2σ) and a larger context.

6. [MAJOR] §6.8: The clean tier is strongly biased (48 % of events at 07–09 h, amplitude KS 0.69). The recommendation still names it the contrastive training set. The plausibility criterion (night/peak 0.0075) is mechanical, as the report concedes. → Do not present it as passing plausibility evidence. Recommend training on SNR ≥ 20 or on all events with SNR as a covariate, and hand this to D as a confound.

7. [MINOR] `inject_eval.py`, injection realism: The templates are high-SNR events scaled down, so the noise in the template is scaled with them. Template selection used the same detector family, and the ranking is only partly robust to that (the BDET/WAVELET sets help). Stretching by 2.5× only approximates distant or slow vehicles, and amplitude is not independent of shape. Recall at SNR ≤ 6 is optimistic. → Say so wherever SNR90 is quoted (SNR90 = 6.6 is a lower bound on true difficulty).

8. [MINOR] `analyze_inj.py`: The operating point is the "most sensitive threshold with FA ≤ 0.2/h" on 24 h of Gaussian surrogates. Zero events in 24 h has a 95 % upper bound of 0.15/h, so the point is noisy. The thresholds differ widely across detectors (HYST needs 10σ, HYST-BP 5σ) because the surrogates ignore non-Gaussian interference. On real data, HYST-BP at that operating point had 0.40 AI-labelled precision, which shows the FA control failed. → State this failure prominently in the summary, not only in §6.2.

9. [MINOR] `tab_bias` / `diurnal.py`: The 1/4 sample is blocks of 6 chunks every 24, with days distributed unevenly (Run 1 contributes 48 % of events). The night/peak CI is a Garwood interval that ignores overdispersion across days and blocks. → Use a bootstrap over days and flag the clustering.

10. [MINOR] `human_pack_labels.csv` / `frozen_params.json`: The HYST-BP surrogate FA on tune is 0.083/h, but the pre-registered threshold sweep grid is coarse (5 vs 5.5). The BDET matched thr = 6 was set after B's native 8 was seen. → Note the grid coarseness.

11. [MINOR] `validate_io.py`: The slot reconstruction disagrees with A's in 5 of 12 chunks (`slots_equal` is False; up to 13 slots per chunk). It is reported as "<1 %", but the resulting time misalignment of up to ~5 ms is not propagated to onset error. `detector.front_end` also uses the median Δt of the ticks, not per-chunk fs. → Note this in the onset-error budget (negligible against the 0.7 s MAE, but should be stated).

12. [MINOR] `detector.py`: `baseline_sigma` applies `binary_dilation(iterations=int(mask_dil_s*FS))` to the whole stream. Edge effects at stream ends are untested (the report admits this) and `median_filter mode="nearest"` biases the baseline at the edges. `has_dropout` events are flagged but the repair function is missing from the deliverable. → Add an edge test, and provide the repair function or remove the recommendation.

13. [MINOR] Reproducibility: The hand-label CSVs are AI inputs and can't be regenerated. `hand_candidates.csv` was regenerated once after a design change. `run_all.sh` order is not verified here, and `.pyc` files are in the tree. → Freeze the label files with hashes.

14. [MINOR] Brief items 2/4: The STA/LTA, CUSUM and WAVELET detectors got fewer tuning iterations than HYST-BP, which received the hp/median additions (partly rebalanced by the -BP variants). There is no template-matching detector. Gap handling at chunk edges is tested only via dropout tests, not via inter-chunk gaps between runs. → Note as a limitation.

## Brief coverage
- C1 evaluation spans across times of day, weekday and weekend, several runs: partial. Weekend is tuning-only, and the holdout is Run 3 only. The spans are 20-min fragments, and there are only 4.0 h tune + 3.3 h holdout.
- C2 ≥4 detectors compared: answered (more than 10).
- C3 label-free evaluation: partial. Injection is answered, and stability is answered. Hand-verified precision uses AI labels and is not blind. Diurnal plausibility is answered but is weak evidence, and cost is answered.
- C4 overlaps, drift, chunk boundaries, gaps: partial. Pairs are resolved only at ≥3 s. Boundaries are handled by stitching. Inter-run gaps are not handled.
- C5 recommendation and `detector.py`: partial. It is delivered, but the tier thresholds are pending human labels, and the window default was changed to 21 s on a lower-bound rule.

## Required follow-ups

2. Reword the Summary and docstring confidence levels (detector "medium", precision "low").
3. Recompute the window rule with a noise-floor cut and ≥30 s context. Then give D both the 12 s and the 21 s options, with the contamination rates.
4. Compute recall at matched real-data FP instead of pooled F1, and separate the detection and segmentation gains.
5. Use a bootstrap over days for the diurnal ratios and rates.
6. Make an edge-effect test on `detect_stream` (drop the first 60 s and compare).
7. Run a fresh-span confirmation: unused Run-3 days plus Run-2 days, human-labelled and at frozen settings, including a weekend.
8. Add a template-independent injection: add real noise-only structure, or use an amplitude sweep with a fixed shape unrelated to any detector.
