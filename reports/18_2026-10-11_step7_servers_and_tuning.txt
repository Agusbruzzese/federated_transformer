4. INTERPRETATION AND CAVEATS (no result about central vs federated)
  - The rule was applied exactly as frozen. Three arm types moved away from
    the M5 default: C256 to 1.5e-4 (+0.047), S+F and CJ to 1e-3 (+0.028,
    +0.025).
  - Each tuning score comes from ONE seed on ONE day. Member B measured a
    seed-x-day residual of about 0.027 for LOBES, so gains of 0.025-0.05
    are within about 1-2 residual SD of noise. The +0.02 threshold limits
    noise-driven switches but cannot remove them. This was a known,
    accepted limitation (reviewer E, m5) and is reported, not corrected.
  - The choices are not obviously asymmetric in favour of one side: the
    reference C256 and the S+F / CJ arms all moved. Every tuning score,
    not only the winner, is in tuned_lr.json for the final report.
  - These Feb 10 scores are tuning data only. Feb 10 is never an
    evaluation day.
