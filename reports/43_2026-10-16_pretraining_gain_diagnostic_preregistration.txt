1. EXPLANATIONS UNDER TEST
  H-data    The server's own pretraining data were noisy (477 low-SNR
            server-day events), so pretraining had little to teach.
  H-lr      S+F's learning rate (1e-3) was tuned on the clean tier; on the
            noisy tier it is wrong (e.g. too high, erasing what
            pretraining gave).
  H-client  Pretraining does not help sites whose data are noisy,
            whatever the server's data and the learning rate.
  These are not exclusive. Each gets its own test and decision rule.

2. DESIGN (low-SNR tier exactly as in report 41; frozen protocol code;
   study commands; same seeds 0-9 x folds 0-2)
  Stage A, re-tuning (H-lr), the study's own procedure:
    F-nat and S+F (noisy server rep10_server_s0), fold 0, seed 0, at the
    study grid 1.5e-4, 3e-4, 6e-4, 1e-3 (8 runs). Scored on the tuning
    day (Feb 10, low-SNR events) with evaluator2.score_tuning. Selection
    by the study rule tune.select: keep 3e-4 unless another lr beats it by
    more than 0.02. -> rep2_tuned.json.
  Stage B, main runs (rep2_manifest.json, generated by rule from stage A):
    Sclean+F   S+F on low-SNR clients, initialised from the STUDY's clean
               servers v21_server_s{seed} (hash-checked), lr 1e-3:
               30 runs (H-data). This is also the realistic deployment:
               the operator pretrains on curated clean data, and the sites
               have whatever quality they have.
    F-nat@lrT / S+F@lrT   only if stage A changes the lr of that arm:
               30 runs each at the re-tuned lr (H-lr). If an arm's lr is
               unchanged, its report-42 runs are that arm.
  Stage C, scoring only: the clean (S0clean) and noisy (S0noisy) server
    encoders alone, scored on every low-SNR fold (descriptive).

3. PRE-REGISTERED DECISIONS (rep2.py analyze; study functions and margins)
  H-data SUPPORTED iff both hold:
    (a) the study's QS rule with Sclean+F in place of S+F returns HELPS:
        Holm family {Sclean+F > F-nat, Sclean+F > F-X+P} x {LOBES,
        POLARITY}, both rejected for one metric with both deltas >= m/2;
    (b) Sclean+F > S+F (noisy server) on LOBES, one-sided p < 0.05
        (two-stratum t).
  H-lr SUPPORTED iff stage A changed at least one lr AND
    S+F@lrT > F-nat@lrT is "HELPS": Holm over LOBES and POLARITY, a
    rejected metric with delta >= m/2. If the study rule keeps both lrs,
    H-lr is REFUTED by that rule without stage-B runs.
  H-client SUPPORTED iff neither H-data nor H-lr is supported.
  Also reported: Sclean+F vs C256 (label); S0clean vs S0noisy (alone);
    mean scores.
