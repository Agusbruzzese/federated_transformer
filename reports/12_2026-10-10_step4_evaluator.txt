1. PURPOSE
  Implement the single scoring and decision procedure used for every arm,
  and prove, BEFORE any federated model exists, that it:
    (a) computes what the protocol says;
    (b) keeps its false-positive rate;
    (c) declares equivalence when two models are truly alike;
    (d) refuses equivalence when a model is degraded, including just past
        the margin;
    (e) detects planted day information.

2. WHAT WAS BUILT (evaluator.py)
  Scoring, per model and fold:
  - Embed the fold's client events and evaluation-day events (eval view).
  - Probes are fitted on the client events and scored per evaluation day:
      kNN       k = 15, StandardScaler
      linear    LogisticRegression(C = 1, lbfgs, float64, tol 1e-8, up to
                10 000 iterations; non-convergence recorded) / RidgeCV;
                StandardScaler
  - Primary: LOBES and POLARITY, each the mean of kNN and linear skill.
    Secondary: DURATION.
  Nuisance-encoding gate:
  - Q = skill(day | [embedding PCA-19, morph8, hour bin, has_dropout])
    - skill(day | [morph8, hour bin, has_dropout]), logistic regression,
    GroupKFold(5) over 30-min blocks, on the fold's 3 evaluation days.
  - Null: 50 block permutations of the day labels. Z = Q - null mean.
  - Unconditioned Z also kept (secondary).
  Statistics:
  - two-stratum t (seed stratum = per-seed means over all days; day stratum
    = day-level differences; Satterthwaite df; 90 % CI);
  - per-metric outcome (EQ / NI / SUP / INC / INF);
  - contrast label (INFERIOR / INCONCLUSIVE / EQUIVALENT / NON-INFERIOR);
  - gate test (Welch t over seeds, one-sided 95 %);
  - margin rule for m_leak; Holm; partition-dependence flag.

3. PASS CRITERIA (written into test_evaluator.py before the first run)
  T1  decision table: every one of 200 000 random CIs gets exactly one
      outcome; boundary cases and the contrast rule as in report 09.
  T2  simulated type I error (P(EQ) at true Delta = +-m) <= 0.07, for LOBES
      and POLARITY, n = 5 vs 5, D = 9, B's variance components.
  T3  Holm on a known example.
  T4  scoring the same checkpoint twice gives identical numbers.
  T5  planted controls on real checkpoints over the 3 folds (9 days):
        C1  central vs independent central       -> not INFERIOR
        C3  untrained init vs central             -> INFERIOR
        C4  central + noise (tau = 0.5 SD)        -> INFERIOR
        C5  central + noise at the margin edge    -> not EQUIVALENT
        L1  gate, central vs central              -> PASS
        L2  planted per-day offset (grid)         -> FAIL for some offset
  T5 is a PROCEDURE TEST ONLY. The checkpoints are M5 models (A = seeds
  0-4), committee B's extra M5-recipe seeds (B = seeds 5-7) and the
  untrained weights of A (I). They were trained on some of these
  evaluation days, so nothing here is a result about central vs federated.

5. FINDINGS TO CARRY FORWARD
  - The procedure behaves as designed on real data: it passes independent
    central models and refuses degraded ones, including a near-margin one.
    This is the proof required before the dry run (step 8).
  - C1 passed even with only 3 seeds in the reference group. The step-8
    dry run (5 vs 5 new seeds, no evaluation-day leakage) is the binding
    version of this control.
  - The gate costs about 15 s per model-fold with 12 threads. Step 11
    (about 175 checkpoints x 3 folds) therefore needs about 2-2.5 h of CPU
    for the gates, within the step-11 estimate.
  - Z sits slightly below 0 for central models. The permutation null of
    the conditional difference is slightly positive (extra features
    overfit noise). Centring on the null mean corrects this, which is why
    the protocol uses Z and not Q.
  - The linear probes converged in every fit checked (no
    ConvergenceWarning).

6. DEVIATIONS FROM PROTOCOL v2
  None. Implementation details not spelled out in report 09, now fixed:
    - day-level skill uses the classes present that day (m4.skill
      convention);
    - PCA-19 of the gate is fitted on the evaluation-day embeddings
      (unsupervised);
    - the hour bin is a 4-level one-hot (6-h bins);
    - the gate permutation seed is 20261010;
    - planted controls use seeded Gaussian noise scaled by the per-dimension
      SD of the client embeddings, and seeded unit vectors per evaluation
      day.
