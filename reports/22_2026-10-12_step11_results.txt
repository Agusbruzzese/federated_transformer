      EQUIVALENT within the margins;

      about one lobe margin lower. This is not a confirmed claim; it is
      flagged, not hidden.

  F-nat (-0.008) suggests averaging, not non-IID data, is where the
  federated arms lose lobe information relative to batch-64 central.
  This is a descriptive pattern only.
