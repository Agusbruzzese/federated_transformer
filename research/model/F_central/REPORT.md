## 3. What was built (`ssl_central/`)

| Module | Contents |
|---|---|
| `config.py` | PREREG constants plus implementation choices I1–I15 (docstring) |
| `data.py` | loading, split, 30-min blocks, P1 targets (`m8_*` per A1; `m_*` secondary), robust noise σ per event, firewall |
| `bgpool.py` | real background pool (I5) |
| `augment.py` | GPU rendering: 12 s crop on an exact 50 Hz grid from `fs_eff`, crop, stretch, amplitude, noise, offset and drift, then max-normalisation; the deterministic evaluation crop; the P3 held-out view |
| `models.py` | CNN (5 conv stages, GroupNorm(8), GELU, GAP, linear → 64); patch transformer (0.5 s patches → 24 tokens, d 64, 4 pre-LN layers, 4 heads, FF 256, mean pool → 64); projection head 64→64→64; NT-Xent with the < 30 s guard |
| `train.py` | AdamW (wd 1e-4), cosine per step, 100 epochs, batch 256 (12 steps per epoch, drop_last); saves the init (= random-init baseline) and the final weights |
| `select.py` | grid, selection on the validation day, seeds |
| `embed.py`, `evaluate.py` | embedding export; P1–P5; verdict |
| `probes.py` | kNN and HGB probes, skill, block bootstrap, group-level permutation, Holm |

**Augmentations**
- Order: random crop → time-stretch U(0.85, 1.18) → amplitude U(0.8, 1.25) → SNR-matched real background → offset U(±0.05·A) plus linear drift U(±0.05·A) → per-view max|x| normalisation.
- Crop rule (I4): the crop contains C's [onset, offset] when it fits (98.4 % of events at any stretch). Otherwise the window centre stays in the middle half of the crop.
- **Not implemented:** sign flip, time reversal, < 4 Hz masking.
- The encoder never sees `log_amp` or SNR.

**Background pool (I5, `logs/bgpool_stdout.txt`)**
- Source: 24 random 41-min blocks from **training days only** (8 per training run, never 0218 or Feb 26), re-run through E's own detection and feature path (`extraction.featpath`).
- 1 930 stretches of 12.8 s were kept, with:
  - no detection of any SNR within ±3 s;
  - no dropout flag;
  - max|x| ≤ 6 robust σ.
- Stretches are stored at unit σ (raw median σ 92 counts).
- Per run: 300 from 0204 (dropout-dense, so fewer clean stretches), 850 from 0212, 780 from 0224. They cover all hours except 05, 13, 19 and 20 h.
- **Noise added:** α·σ_ev with α ~ U(0, 1), where σ_ev is the robust σ of the event's own window outside [onset, offset] ±1 s. The view's noise floor therefore stays within √2 of the event's own.

**Evaluation crop (I3).** Deterministic central 12 s crop, stretch 1, centred on the window centre (C's energy centroid), resampled to exactly 50 Hz. The same crop is used for the train, validation and holdout embeddings.

   - caching;
   - the tier budget in item 3;
   - a table-rendering fallback (`to_string`, because `tabulate` is not installed).

   No target, split, criterion, probe, metric or selection rule was changed. `config.py` is hash-identical to training start.
3. **Secondary-tier P2 was reduced** under the orchestrator's compute budget (check-in, 2026-09-30):
   - only the 6 SSL models and morph8 (the representations the P2 criterion uses);
   - 5 permutations at SNR ≥ 30 and 2 at all-SNR, instead of ≥ 20.

   The primary tier uses 20 permutations for all 16 representations.
4. **HGB early stopping at all-SNR.** sklearn's `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` defaults to `early_stopping='auto'`, which turns on for n > 10 000. It is therefore active **only** in the all-SNR tier (15 260 events), with a 10 % internal validation split. E's probe had the same default, so this matches E's all-SNR numbers.
5. **Secondary tiers were re-evaluated, not retrained (I15).** The PREREG says "repeat P1 and P2" without saying whether to retrain. E recommends against all-SNR training without a blip filter.
6. **Interpretations fixed a priori** in `config.py` where the PREREG is silent:
   - P2 probe = E's HGB (I13); kNN is secondary.
   - P2 pass is decided on the seed-mean point estimate.
   - The P1 p-value is a one-sided bootstrap-percentile p (I12).
   - kNN uses z-scored features (I9).
   - The random-init baseline is each seed's own initial weights (I11).
   - The final seed 0 reuses the grid run (I10).
   - Crop, noise and drift parameters: I4–I6.
   - Evaluation crop: I3.
7. **P3 held-out augmentation.** Its components lie inside the training ranges: 0.9× is within U(0.85, 1.18), and ±0.5 s is within the crop jitter. Only the fixed combination without noise is "held out". It was implemented exactly as the PREREG specifies.
8. **Parameter matching is on the encoder** (210 592 vs 207 424, −1.5 %). The projection head (8 320 parameters) is identical and excluded from the count.
9. **Human-pack check skipped**, because there are no labels.
10. **Retrieval metric choices.** P3 uses cosine; P4 and P5 use z-scored Euclidean. P5 bootstrap ARI uses 20 block resamples. The PREREG does not specify these.

## 11. Limitations

1. **Amplitude comes via the noise floor.** log_amp from max-normalised input equals the SNR-alone predictor (R² 0.82 vs 0.79 / 0.77). The log_amp P1 "pass" should be read as SNR leakage, not as recognising vehicle size. With log_amp discounted:
   - the CNN has only 2 targets of real improvement, polarity and n_lobes, since log_dur got worse;
   - the transformer has 3.
2. **The CNN loses duration.** log_dur is worse than random-init at SNR ≥ 20 (−0.046) and at SNR ≥ 30 (−0.137). This is the expected price of invariance to U(0.85, 1.18) time-stretch plus crop jitter, which the augmentation design asks for (vehicle-speed invariance). It shows the embedding is partly speed-invariant by construction. The transformer keeps log_dur above its random-init at SNR ≥ 20.
3. **Weak holdout and small samples.**
   - Run 0218 was seen once by C.
   - There are 93 holdout blocks with skewed sizes.
   - Selection used one validation day of 366 events; CNN grid means span only 0.587–0.639.
4. **PCA is handicapped by z-scoring (I9).** z-scoring the 64 components inflates the low-variance ones in the kNN metric. The PCA-64 baseline (b) is therefore weaker than an unscaled PCA would be. This was fixed a priori and not changed.
5. **Run 0204 is identifiable.** Run-0204 identifiability rises from random-init (0.08 / 0.03) to SSL (0.17 / 0.11). It is below morphology but not zero. It matters for a date- or run-based federated split.
6. **Non-vehicles in the tier.** The SNR ≥ 20 tier contains non-vehicles (E §4c). No human labels exist.
7. **P5 shows no cluster structure**, so a clustering-based downstream use is unsupported.
8. **Single training budget.** Each architecture got one fixed budget (100 epochs). No claim is made about longer training.
