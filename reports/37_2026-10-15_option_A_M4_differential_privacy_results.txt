SUMMARY
-------
The DP mechanism is implemented correctly and the simulator used to
measure it is proven exact against the real system (V1, V2: bit-identical).
The pre-registered prediction is confirmed. With 10 sites there is NO
setting of site-level DP that keeps the model equivalent or non-inferior to
plain training (z* = None):
  - privacy so weak that the guarantee is meaningless (epsilon >= 1e5)
    still costs about 0.024 on LOBES;
  - clipping alone already costs 0.030;
  - at epsilon <= 1311 the model ends WORSE than the pretrained server
    model it started from;
  - at a meaningful epsilon (6.6) it has lost 0.35 LOBES and 0.25
    POLARITY relative to that starting point.
Site-level DP is therefore NOT recommended for this federation of 10
sites. SecAgg+ (M3) with TLS + authentication (M2) is the recommended
protection.

5. INTERPRETATION
  a. The prediction of report 35 holds: utility is lost from about
     z = 0.1, and at every epsilon that would count as privacy the
     training destroys the model. Correction to report 35 sec. 2: with the
     full recipe the noise/signal factor is about 79*z (n_max/sum(n) =
     2176/12730), not 77*z (that figure used the 3-round test recipe's
     896/5412). The conclusion is unchanged.
  b. The cause is structural, not a tuning problem. Site-level DP noise
     must hide one whole site out of 10, i.e. about 17 % of each round's
     weighted update. Noise of that size spread over 215 744 weights
     outweighs the signal unless epsilon is astronomically large. DP-FedAvg
     is designed for thousands of participants, where one participant's
     share is tiny.
  c. Clipping costs utility on its own (-0.030 LOBES at z = 0; CI just
     reaches past the margin). Clipping at the median was fixed in
     advance; a higher clip norm would cost less here, but it would raise
     the noise needed for the same epsilon in exact proportion. It cannot
     change conclusion (b).
  d. Limits: 3 seeds, 1 fold, 3 evaluation days (wide CIs, several
     INCONCLUSIVE labels); no other clipping norm, no adaptive clipping,
     no tuning of the learning rate under noise. None of these can close a
     gap of 0.35-0.45 LOBES at epsilon <= 30.

6. VERDICT AND RECOMMENDATION
  M4 implementation: CORRECT and EXACT (U1-U4, V1, V2 PASS).
  M4 utility: site-level DP is NOT usable for a 10-site federation of this
  model. No privacy level keeps the model equivalent to plain training, and
  every meaningful privacy level makes it worse than no federated training
  at all.
  Recommendation: deploy with TLS + site authentication (M2) and SecAgg+
  (M3). Do not enable site-level DP. If a formal privacy guarantee is
  required, the realistic routes are new designs, not settings:
    - record-level DP inside each site (DP-SGD with a loss whose
      per-example sensitivity is bounded);
    - many more sites.
  Either would be a separate pre-registered study.
